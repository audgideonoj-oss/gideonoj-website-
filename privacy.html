Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Aud. Gideon OJ respects the privacy of visitors and clients who use this website and our professional services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use the Gideon Business Health Check website or engage our professional services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect information such as your name, business name, contact details and financial information voluntarily provided by you.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant assessment or professional service.

3. Business Health Check Information

Information entered into the Business Health Check may include business revenue, costs, expenses, cash, receivables and creditors.

This information is used to calculate and present the financial health assessment requested by the user.

4. How We Use Information

Information provided by users may be used to:

  • Provide the Business Health Check assessment.
  • Prepare requested professional reports.
  • Communicate with clients about requested services.
  • Provide accounting, auditing and financial advisory services.
  • Improve our services and website.

5. Payment Information

Payments for professional services may be processed through approved third-party payment providers.

Aud. Gideon OJ does not intend to collect or store users' complete card details through this website. Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider in accordance with its own security and privacy policies.

6. Confidentiality

Information supplied by clients for professional engagements will be treated as confidential, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed where necessary to provide a requested service, process a payment, comply with legal requirements or protect our legitimate rights and interests.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information supplied to us against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal or professional obligations.

10. Your Rights

Where applicable, you may request information about personal data held about you and may request correction of inaccurate information.

Requests concerning personal information should be made using the contact details provided on this website.

11. Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services such as payment processors or communication platforms. Such services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Aud. Gideon OJ through the contact details provided on this website.
