Refund Policy

Aud. Gideon OJ

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Aud. Gideon OJ is committed to providing professional accounting, auditing, financial analysis and business advisory services.

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which payments made for our professional services may be refunded.

2. Business Health Check

The Gideon Business Health Check is an automated assessment based on information supplied by the user.

Where the Business Health Check is provided free of charge, there is no payment to refund for the assessment itself.

3. Professional Services

Payments made for professional services are generally non-refundable once substantial professional work has commenced.

This may include financial reviews, business analysis, accounting support, internal control reviews, revenue assurance and other professional advisory services.

4. Cancellation Before Work Begins

A client may request cancellation of a professional service before substantial work has commenced.

Where appropriate, Aud. Gideon OJ may issue a full or partial refund depending on the circumstances and any work already performed.

5. Service Delays

Where a service cannot reasonably be delivered due to circumstances within our control, the client may be offered an alternative service date or, where appropriate, a refund.

6. Incorrect or Duplicate Payments

If a client believes that an incorrect or duplicate payment has been made, the client should contact Aud. Gideon OJ as soon as possible with the relevant payment details.

Verified duplicate or erroneous payments may be refunded through the appropriate payment channel.

7. Client Responsibility

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information and required documents for professional engagements.

Refunds will not normally be issued solely because a client fails to provide information required to complete an agreed service.

8. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the original payment method or another appropriate payment method agreed with the client.

9. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact Aud. Gideon OJ using the contact details provided on this website.

Please include your name, business name, payment date, amount paid and a brief explanation of the reason for the refund request.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
