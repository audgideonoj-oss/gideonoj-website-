Frequently Asked Questions

Aud. Gideon OJ

1. What is the Gideon Business Health Check?

The Gideon Business Health Check is a simple financial assessment designed to help business owners understand important areas of their business, including profitability, expense control, liquidity, working capital and receivables.

2. Is the Business Health Check free?

Yes. The basic Business Health Check is available as a free assessment. It provides a general financial health score based on the information entered by the user.

3. What information do I need to complete the assessment?

You will generally need information such as your business revenue, costs, operating expenses, available cash, customer receivables and supplier obligations.

4. How is my business health score calculated?

The assessment considers five major areas: profitability, expense control, liquidity, working capital and receivables management. These areas are combined to produce a score out of 100.

5. Can I download my assessment report?

Yes. After completing the assessment, users can generate and download a Business Health Assessment report containing the score, financial observations, risk indicators, priority area and recommended actions.

6. Is the Business Health Check a professional audit?

No. The Business Health Check is a preliminary financial assessment and should not be regarded as a statutory audit, financial statement audit or substitute for a complete professional engagement.

7. What professional services are available?

Aud. Gideon OJ provides professional accounting, auditing, financial analysis, revenue assurance, financial controls and business advisory services.

Clients may also request a Quick Financial Review, Detailed Business Review or Comprehensive Financial Review.

8. How much do the professional reviews cost?

The current service options displayed on the Business Health Check website include:

  • Quick Financial Review — ₦5,000
  • Detailed Business Review — ₦15,000
  • Comprehensive Financial Review — ₦25,000

The scope of work and applicable fee will be confirmed with the client before the professional engagement begins.

9. How do I request a professional review?

After completing the Business Health Check, you can select the professional service you require and follow the instructions provided to contact Aud. Gideon OJ.

10. How do I make payment?

Where payment is required, available approved payment options will be communicated to the client. Payment instructions should only be followed through the official channels provided by Aud. Gideon OJ.

11. Will my business information be kept confidential?

Information provided for professional services will be treated as confidential, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

12. Can I request a refund?

Refund requests are considered in accordance with our Refund Policy. Where substantial professional work has already commenced, payments will generally not be refundable.

13. Who can use the Business Health Check?

The assessment is designed for business owners, entrepreneurs and other users who want a simple overview of important financial areas of a business.

14. What if I need a more detailed analysis?

You can request a professional review after completing the assessment. A detailed engagement can examine your records and provide more specific findings and recommendations based on the agreed scope.

15. How can I contact Aud. Gideon OJ?

You can use the contact details and communication options provided on this website to request information or professional services.
